Orlog

The Viking dice game of fate. Reduce your rival's fifteen health stones to zero.

How the game works
  • Each player has 15 health stones. Reduce your opponent to zero to win. If both fall together the round ends in a draw.
  • Roll phase. Each player has six dice and up to three rolls a round, alternating turns. After a roll, tap dice to keep them. Kept dice are not rolled again.
  • Dice. Axes deal melee damage and helmets block them. Arrows deal ranged damage and shields block them. Hands steal one favor token each. Dice with a gold border earn you one favor token each.
  • Favor phase. Spend tokens to call on one god for the round, at one of three tiers. Both players choose before anything resolves.
  • Resolution. Costs are paid, favors apply, then axes meet helmets, arrows meet shields, and hands steal tokens. Unspent tokens carry over. The roller order swaps every round.

Each die has six faces: gold axe, axe, gold arrow, helmet, gold shield, hand. Favor costs and effects here are my own tuning, so they differ from the video game version.
